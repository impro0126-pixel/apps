Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Read Anything does not collect your personal information. There is no account, and the developer runs no server. The documents and settings you add stay on your devices.

1. What we don't collect

The app collects none of the following.

  • Identity information such as your name, email or phone number (there is no sign-up)
  • Location
  • Contacts, or access to your whole photo library
  • Usage or analytics data (the app contains no analytics or ad modules)
  • Device identifiers

2. What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is not sent anywhere.

WhatWhere
Text, titles, collections and listening position of what you addThe app's documents folder
Original PDFs and photos (used to extract text again)The app's documents folder
Downloaded audio filesThe app's support files folder
Settings such as pronunciation fixes, skipped lines and speedThe app's settings storage
OpenAI and Fish Audio API keysThe device's secure storage (Keychain)

If you use syncing between devices, the documents and settings above move to your own iCloud (the same Apple Account). The developer cannot see them. API keys are not synced.

Deleting the app removes this data from the device. To delete it within the app, delete each item, or clear downloaded audio, pronunciation fixes and skipped lines in Settings.

3. When something leaves your device — only when you choose

The app has no server run by the developer. There is no path for your data to reach the developer.

However, if you add your own OpenAI or Fish Audio API key and choose that feature, the data below is sent directly to that company under your own account.

FeatureWhat is sentWhen
Reading with an OpenAI voiceThe text of the sentences being readWhen the voice engine is set to OpenAI
Extracting text with OpenAIImages of the document or photoWhen you choose that method or tap “Re-extract”
Reading with a Fish Audio voiceThe text of the sentences being readWhen the voice engine is set to Fish Audio
  • This traffic does not pass through the developer. The developer cannot see what is sent.
  • Charges go directly to your OpenAI or Fish Audio account.
  • Data sent is handled under OpenAI's privacy policy and Fish Audio's privacy policy.
  • By default, everything is processed on your device for free. These features work only when you turn them on in Settings.
  • For sensitive documents such as contracts or medical records, we recommend turning off OpenAI and Fish Audio and using on-device processing only.

4. Device permissions

PermissionWhy
PhotosTo read the photos you pick yourself. The app does not scan your whole library.
FilesTo read the files you pick or drag in yourself.

Both apply only to the items you choose at that moment.

5. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not share or sell any data to third parties, because no data is collected. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

7. Changes to this policy

If anything changes, this page and its date are updated. If a change would collect more, we will tell you separately in the app.
