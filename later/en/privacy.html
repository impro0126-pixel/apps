Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Keepshelf does not collect your personal information. There is no account, and the developer runs no server. The photos, links and notes you save stay only on your devices and in your own iCloud.

1. What we don't collect

The app collects none of the following:

  • Identity information such as your name, email or phone number (there is no sign-up)
  • Location
  • Contacts, or access to your whole photo library
  • Usage or analytics data (the app contains no analytics or advertising code)
  • Device identifiers
  • Face ID or Touch ID data (see section 4)

2. Where your data is kept

WhatWhere
The photos, links and notes you save, and each card's title, note and folderThe app's own storage on your device
The same items (to show them on all your devices)Your private iCloud storage
Cards in the Trash (deleted automatically after 30 days)Same as above
Backups and exports you makeOnly the folder you choose

Your private iCloud storage is kept by Apple in your own account; the developer cannot see its contents. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. Data left in iCloud can be removed in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud on your device.

3. When anything leaves your device

The app has no developer-operated server. There is no path by which your data reaches the developer.

FeatureWhat goes where
Link previewsTo get a link's title and preview image, your device connects directly to that link's website (or that site's own public preview service, such as YouTube's). It is the same as opening the page in a browser.
Sync across devicesGoes to your own iCloud (section 2).
Text in photosRead on your device by Apple's built-in text recognition. Photos are not uploaded anywhere for this.
“Listen in Read Anything”Hands the card to the Read Anything app on the same device. It does not leave the device.
Share buttonSends only to the place you choose (Photos, Messages and so on).

4. Device permissions

PermissionWhy it's needed
Choosing photosOnly the photos you pick at that moment are saved. The app does not scan your library.
Adding to PhotosUsed only when you tap “Save Image” to put that photo into Photos. You're asked once the first time.
Face ID / Touch IDUsed only to open folders you have locked. The check is done by your device; the app never receives your face or fingerprint data.

5. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not provide or sell any data to third parties, because no data is collected. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone of any age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated. If a change would widen what is collected, you will be told inside the app.
