Keepshelf

A scrapbook for screenshots, photos, links and notes

Download on the App Store

What it is

A place to keep the things you want to look at again: movie posters, album covers, screenshots of lines worth rereading, photos and links. Everything stacks up in a grid like a scrapbook, so you can browse it all at a glance later.

  • Save from any app with one tap on Share. Several photos at once work too
  • Links get a preview image automatically
  • Sort cards into folders and search them, including the text inside your photos
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac see the same collection through your own iCloud
  • No sign-up, no login, no ads

Frequently asked

I can't find Keepshelf in the Share menu

On iPhone, scroll the row of apps in the Share menu all the way to the right and turn it on under “More”. On Mac, open System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions, scroll to “Extensions”, choose “By Category”, then click ⓘ next to “Sharing” and turn on Keepshelf. macOS turns off sharing for newly installed apps at first.

Sharing from the Photos app on Mac doesn't add the photo

On Mac, select the photos in Photos and drag them onto the Keepshelf window. Several at once work too.

What I saved on iPhone doesn't show up on my Mac

Check that both devices are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account. Keep the app open and the cards arrive shortly. Photos can take a little longer than text because of their size.

How do I move or delete several cards at once?

Tap “Select” at the top, choose the cards, then use “Move to Folder” or the Trash button in the bar below. On Mac you can also use ⌘-click, Shift-click and ⌘A.

I deleted something by mistake

Open the Trash at the end of the folder chips to restore it. Cards stay in the Trash for 30 days.

I forgot how to open a locked folder

Locked folders open with Face ID or Touch ID (or your device passcode). Only you can open them on your device; the developer has no way to unlock them.

How do I put a saved photo back into Photos?

Open the card, tap the Share button at the top and choose “Save Image” (“Photos” on Mac). The first time, you'll be asked whether it can add to Photos. The card stays in Keepshelf.

The app is in Korean. How do I switch to English?

Keepshelf follows your device's first language. To choose yourself, go to Settings → View → Language in the app. The change applies the next time you open the app.

What is “Listen in Read Anything”?

It sends a card to Read Anything, another app from the same developer, so you can hear it read aloud. If you don't have that app, its App Store page opens. Keepshelf works fully without it.

Where does what I save go?

Only to your devices and your own iCloud. Nothing is sent to the developer's servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.
